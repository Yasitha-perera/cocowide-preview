About Us

Excelling in growing media

Growing Sustenance through Innovation and Nature.

At Cocowide, we transform the natural potential of renewable coco peat into world- class growing solutions. As an industry leader and technical expert, we empower the global greenhouse sector by delivering substrates that consistently outperform traditional media in both sustainability and agricultural results.

Uncompromising Quality at the Source

Superior performance begins with the raw material. Our Sri Lankan manufacturing arms—Victory Line (Pvt) Ltd and Growmax (Pvt) Ltd—are strategically positioned in the heart of the “Coconut Triangle.” This location allows us to capture the island’s finest raw materials, ensuring a consistent and premium supply for our global clientele.

Strategic Global Expansion

To meet the rising global demand for specialized products, we have expanded our footprint into India through Cocowide Exports Pvt Ltd. This venture is strengthened by our partnership with Coir Brothers Eco Supplies LLP, whose deep heritage in the Indian coir industry perfectly complements Cocowide’ s recognized market expertise. By leveraging this combined strength, we specialize in high-precision low-EC and washed products. From the traditional plantations of Sri Lanka and India, we provide a comprehensive range of solutions designed for the world’s most demanding professional growers.

Strategic Direction

Our Vision

"To be the most sorted after supplier of value-added coconut-based substrates for the selective niche markets which can generate adequate customer-value in return.”

Our Mission

"To develop mutually beneficial long-term business relationships with the potential clients mainly in markets which need personalized and customized value-added coconut-based substrates products. To grasp special needs and wants of niche markets which have been oblivious to much suppliers and develop products to successfully match that demand.”

Corporate Values

As a manufacturer of renewable materials, the company emphasizes a commitment to a sustainable planet. We are also dedicated to protecting coconut farms and improving the well-being of local farming communities, which they consider vital to their business success. The company emphasizes delivering exactly what was agreed upon, acting as a reliable, long-term partner in the supply chain.
