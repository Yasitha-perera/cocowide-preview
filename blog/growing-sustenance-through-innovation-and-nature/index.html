Growing Sustenance through Innovation and Nature.

At Cocowide, we transform the natural potential of renewable coco peat into world- class growing solutions. As an industry leader and technical expert, we empower the global greenhouse sector by delivering substrates that consistently outperform traditional media in both sustainability and agricultural results.

Uncompromising Quality at the Source

Superior performance begins with the raw material. Our Sri Lankan manufacturing arms—Victory Line (Pvt) Ltd and Growmax (Pvt) Ltd—are strategically positioned in the heart of the “Coconut Triangle.” This location allows us to capture the island’s finest raw materials, ensuring a consistent and premium supply for our global clientele.
