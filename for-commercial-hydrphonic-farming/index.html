FOR COMMERCIAL HYDRPHONIC FARMING

Coco/coir has been long identified as an excellent growing medium for hydroponic framing. Generally coco/coir is known as Coconut Based Substrates or Coconut Based Growing media which are becoming increasingly popular among professional growers worldwide as an alternative to the traditional peat moss, stone wool and various other potting mixes due to its superior water holding capacity and air filled porosity. These main properties maintain ideal water, oxygen and nutrient availability to the root system of the plants. Cocowide has developed its range of Grow Bags and Open Top products after carefully studying crop requirements and different conditions under which they are grown.
